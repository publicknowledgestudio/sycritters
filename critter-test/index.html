A little critter lives on the top edge of this box. Here's what it does — tap a button below to trigger any animation.
Follows — when your cursor comes within ~100px it walks over, speeding up as it gets closer.
Leaps — from a short distance it jumps toward your cursor along a natural gravity arc to close the gap.
Catches you — when it reaches your cursor it celebrates with a random hop, wiggle, dance or nod.
Shimmers — a light sweep washes over it while you type in the composer.
Celebrates — a happy hop when you press Enter.
Rests & sleeps — sits at home (top-right), blinks every ~10s, and dozes off if you leave it alone for a while.
Eyes — its two pupils point toward your cursor: a 2px bar for left/right/up/down, or a 1px corner for diagonals.
Performance — an 8×8 sprite stepped at 12fps. The loop parks itself when nothing is happening, so cost is ~0 while idle or asleep, and well under a millisecond per frame when active. Click it (the gear) to restyle.
